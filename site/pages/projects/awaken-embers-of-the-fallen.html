Overview

When dimensional Tears begin to open across the world, elite special-ops unit Alpha Team is drawn into a hidden cosmic conflict. Ancient beings known as Elysians uphold a sacred covenant, while the Severed seek to unleash Armageddon. Bound by prophecy and hunted by forces beyond human comprehension, Alpha Team must uncover the truth before humanity becomes collateral in a celestial war.
